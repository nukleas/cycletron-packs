# Winter, 3D

A visualizer pack (see `docs/PACKS.md` in the Cycletron repo): four three.js modes (a band whose hits land on the frame the note sounds, the same cave as a DJ set, a penguin headlining a warehouse, and the aurora) and the `glacier` palette. `dist/index.js` is what the app loads; it imports only `@cycletron/viz-sdk` and bundles its own copy of three. The sources live in the cycletron-packs repo (`src/modes/<mode>/` per mode, `src/lib/` for what the modes share, and the repo's `kit/` for what the packs share, including the one shared WebGL renderer), which builds and zips this folder; an installed copy carries only the build.

Ice cave is a band in an ice cavern. A yeti plays a two-octave marimba of crystals: every pitched note lights its crystal in the track's colour, and the yeti's mallets, raised over the next crystal an eighth of a bar before, land on the mid and lead notes. Kicks are the yeti's stomp, the bass lights two crystal pillars, and a penguin plays the snares on an ice drum and the hats on icicle chimes. A drop of meltwater swells a step a beat through each phrase's last bar and splashes on the downbeat. The camera cuts on the bar lines.

Yeti DJ is the same cave after hours (the cave and both figures are shared, in `src/lib/`). The yeti, in sunglasses, plays two turntables on an ice booth, and a laser rig over the decks is the pitched notes: each pitch has its own beam, across the fan by pitch class and tilted by register, lit in the track's colour on the onset and held while the note holds. The yeti's lenses catch the brightest beam. Kicks nod the yeti and light the booth, snares scratch the left deck and throw the penguin's flippers up, hats hop the penguin, and the right hand rides the mixer with the leads. Each phrase's last bar the yeti's arm climbs a step a beat, and on the downbeat it punches up and every beam fires white. Around him is a show: a riser whose lip flashes on the kick, a video wall with a column per pitch class lit to how loud that pitch is, a truss of four spots that step to new marks on each beat and flare on the kick, cryo jets on the drop, and a crowd of penguins in silhouette that hops on the kick and puts its flippers up for the snares and the drop.

Penguin warehouse puts the penguin on stage as the headliner, between two banks of twelve hex pads, a pitch class per pad (below C4 on the left bank, C4 and up on the right). Its flippers wind up over the next note's pad and touch it on the onset, and a held note keeps its pad and a raised outline lit. Kicks plant its foot, press the stage down, pump the speaker cones and fire the overhead strobes, which wash the stage white. Snares crouch the crowd and throw its flippers up as one, and hats chase lights around the halo behind it. The cameras cut on the bar lines: close, low from the pads, over the crowd, and wide for the phrase's last bar.

The ice cave, the DJ booth and the warehouse also draw the song's glitches, from its notes (`src/lib/glitch.ts`, drawn by `glitch-fx.ts`). A stutter, a track firing 32nds or faster, loops the picture a sixteenth of a bar at a time and jumps the camera on each loop. A choke, nothing sounding for half a beat to a bar, cracks ice over the lens until the next onset shatters it. A masked long note still sounds its whole length, so a hole has to be really empty to crack.

Aurora is the northern lights over a cabin on a snowy ridge. Five curtains share the pitched tracks; a note lights its curtain at its pitch and the light spreads while the note is held, so pads burn the whole sky and short notes flick. Highs shimmer the rays, kicks surge light up the curtains, snares are shooting stars, hats twinkle the stars, bass warms the window, and the snow takes the aurora's colour.

Geometry and materials are built once; `layout()` recolours them from the theme. Notes come from `HapField`; spawns run in `update()`, which also places everything, and `render()` only hands the scene to the shared renderer.

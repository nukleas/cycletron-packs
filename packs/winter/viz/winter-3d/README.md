# Winter, 3D

A visualizer pack (see `docs/PACKS.md` in the Cycletron repo): four three.js modes, three of them performers whose hits land on the frame the note sounds, and the `glacier` palette. `dist/index.js` is what the app loads; it imports only `@cycletron/viz-sdk` and bundles its own copy of three. The sources live in the cycletron-packs repo (`src/modes/<mode>/` per mode, `src/lib/` for what the modes share, and the repo's `kit/` for what the packs share, including the one shared WebGL renderer), which builds and zips this folder; an installed copy carries only the build.

Ice cave is a band in an ice cavern. A yeti plays a two-octave marimba of crystals: every pitched note lights its crystal in the track's colour, and the yeti's mallets, raised over the next crystal an eighth of a bar before, land on the mid and lead notes. Kicks are the yeti's stomp, the bass lights two crystal pillars, and a penguin plays the snares on an ice drum and the hats on icicle chimes. A drop of meltwater swells a step a beat through each phrase's last bar and splashes on the downbeat. The camera cuts on the bar lines.

Snowball fight is two snow forts with four kids each, one voice per kid: kicks, snares and two pitched tracks on the left; hats, other percussion and two more pitched tracks on the right. A kid cocks, throws, and the ball splats on the opposite wall on the onset; the kid behind it ducks. Pitch is the height of the arc. A snowman goes up a ball a bar through each phrase, counts the last bar's beats with its arms and bursts on the downbeat. The camera cuts on the bar lines.

Aurora is the northern lights over a cabin on a snowy ridge. Five curtains share the pitched tracks; a note lights its curtain at its pitch and the light spreads while the note is held, so pads burn the whole sky and short notes flick. Highs shimmer the rays, kicks surge light up the curtains, snares are shooting stars, hats twinkle the stars, bass warms the window, and the snow takes the aurora's colour.

Ski jump is a floodlit jump at night, one jumper a bar: the in-run, the lip on beat three, the landing on the next downbeat, with the jumper's tuck, pop, V, brace and telemark switched on those beats. The bar's highest note sets the distance and a line marks the spot. Kicks throw snow from the skis, the lamps along the hill light by pitch, held notes brighten the floodlights, snares are camera flashes in the crowd. The cameras cut like a broadcast.

Geometry and materials are built once; `layout()` recolours them from the theme. Notes come from `HapField`; spawns run in `update()`, which also places everything, and `render()` only hands the scene to the shared renderer.

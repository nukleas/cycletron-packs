# New Year's Eve, 3D

A visualizer pack (see `docs/PACKS.md` in the Cycletron repo): four three.js modes and the `midnight` palette. `dist/index.js` is what the app loads; it imports only `@cycletron/viz-sdk` and carries its own copy of three. The sources live in the cycletron-packs repo (`src/modes/<mode>/` per mode, `src/lib/` for what the modes share, and the repo's `kit/` for what the packs share), which builds and zips this folder; an installed copy carries only the build.

Fireworks is a harbour skyline. Every pitched note is a shell that launches a little over a second early, so it bursts on its onset: pitch is height, the register picks the shell (bass willows on the beat, mid chrysanthemums, lead peonies), long notes burst wider. Snares are white salutes, crashes rings, kicks barge fountains; hats make the stars crackle. Bursts light the haze and the upper floors in their colour, and the water carries a dim mirror of the sky.

Ball drop is a mirror ball on a tower mast. It slides down across every eight-bar phrase while the display counts the beats left, lands on the next downbeat (00, a white flash and a shower of sparks) and climbs back up over the first beat. Its facets are LED panels: a note lights the ring at its pitch's latitude in its track colour. The billboards follow one track each; kicks flash the ball and the searchlights.

Confetti is a party crew on a stage, one guest per voice, posed in a few fixed stances that switch on the onsets. The cannon gunner aims an eighth of a bar before each kick and fires on it; two popper guests split the snares (popper up before, string yanked on the hit); the blower guest's party blower unrolls on each hat; the thrower crouches a quarter bar before a crash and leaps with a double handful on it. Four sparkler guests on the riser each play one pitched track: the sparkler comes up before the line, goes overhead on each note and sparks while it holds. Four lamps over the stage count the beats of each four-bar phrase's last bar and the ceiling net drops on the downbeat. The camera cuts every two bars.

Champagne is a tower of fourteen coupes on a round table with the guests around it. The sommelier lifts the bottle an eighth of a bar before each bar line and pours for the first quarter of the bar; a full glass spills into the four under it, so the tower fills from the top and then cascades. The cork popper fires a cork on each kick, two toasters split the snares (glass raised high on the hit), a guest taps a coupe with a spoon on each hat, and the sabreur raises the sabre a quarter bar before a crash and takes the neck off on it. Pitched notes light the tower's glasses by pitch. A wall clock runs the last sixteen minutes to midnight a beat at a time through each four-bar phrase and strikes twelve on the downbeat. The camera cuts every two bars.

All four draw through `@naderlabs/kit/gl`'s shared renderer. Sparks, bubbles and lights are one pooled `Points` buffer per mode (`lib/glow.ts`); confetti is one `InstancedMesh`. The guests are one instanced rig (`lib/figures.ts`) posed from `@naderlabs/kit/timing`: each voice's last onset and next onset give its stance. Shells, sparks and the landing shower are closed-form paths from an onset and a seed; confetti and the champagne levels are stepped in `update()`. `render()` only turns state into buffers. Colours come from the theme in `layout()`.

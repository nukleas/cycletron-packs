# Harvest, 3D

A visualizer pack (see `docs/PACKS.md` in the Cycletron repo): four three.js modes and the `harvest` palette, low-poly and flat-shaded. `dist/index.js` is what the app loads; it imports only `@cycletron/viz-sdk` and bundles its own copy of three. The sources live in the cycletron-packs repo (`src/modes/<mode>/` per mode, `src/lib/` for what the modes share, and the repo's `kit/` for what the packs share, `gl` above all), which builds and zips this folder; an installed copy carries only the build.

Autumn forest is a woodcutters' clearing where every voice has a worker, posed through the kit's `timing` (at rest, readied on the wind-up, in contact on the onset frame). Kicks are the woodsman at the chopping block, whose axe is overhead an eighth of a bar before each kick and in the log on it, the log splitting; snares the hatchet at the kindling block; hats a woodpecker; crashes the cabin's dinner bell. Three maples play bass, mid and lead, gathering before each note, swelling and dropping leaves in the track colour on it, and wearing that colour while it holds. Two sawyers count the phrase: a saw stroke every beat, the tree leaning a step per beat in the last bar of four and hitting the ground on the next downbeat. The camera cuts between four fixed shots every two bars, offset a bar so the count and the fall share a shot.

Parade is a marching band halted at the reviewing stand. The drum line plays one voice each (bass drum on kicks, two snare drummers trading the snares, the cymbals on crashes), each horn player has a pitched track and kicks the bell up on its notes, and the drum major counts the last bar of every phrase and thrusts the mace on the downbeat. The band marks time on the beat, the camera cuts every two bars, and one balloon float crosses per phrase, dipping on the beat.

Cornfield is a field at sunset round a scarecrow. A wave rolls across the rows every two bars and gusts on each beat, every kick sends a ring of wind out from the scarecrow and flaps its arms, its head nods on the beat, snares put up crows, crashes flush the two on its arms, hats puff pollen off the tassels. Pitched notes light ears of corn, pitch picking the row. The camera cuts between four places every two bars.

Harvest moon is a pumpkin patch under a rising moon, played like a keyboard: bass, mid and lead rows of twelve, one per pitch class, each lit from inside in the track colour while its note holds. Kicks pulse the moon's halo, hats spark fireflies, snares and crashes send shooting stars, the bass lights the barn's loft window. The camera cuts between four places every two bars.

Each mode builds its scene once, writes instance matrices and colours from its own state in update(), and only calls the kit's shared renderer in render(). Every InstancedMesh has its own material: two instanced meshes sharing one made a render that starts mid-song differ by a level or two on a few edge pixels from one that played through. Instanced meshes also skip frustum culling (`Stage.uncull`), since three computes their bounds once, at the first draw, and instances that move on would be culled by those stale bounds.
